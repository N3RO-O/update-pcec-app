What it does

What members can do today

Everything below works in the testing app. Only approved PCEC members can see member content.

  • Join easily. Sign up with email or Google, and stay signed in.
  • Keep up with church life. News, events with registration and calendar, and notifications.
  • Share and react. Posts with photos, reactions and comments, for everyone, friends only or just you.
  • Talk together. A community chat room and private group chats by invitation.
  • Pray for one another. Prayer requests, anonymous if wanted, with "praying" counts.
  • Find help and churches. Resources and a directory of member churches.
  • Give. PCEC's official payment channels and QR codes. The app doesn't handle money.
  • Install it like an app. Scan a QR code; no app store needed yet.

Decisions

Five decisions only PCEC can make

These move the launch forward.

  1. Official web address and sender email

    For example app.pcec.org.ph, and a no-reply address on PCEC's domain.

  2. Pilot group

    10–30 members with a mix of Android phones and iPhones.

  3. Support contact

    The person who answers account and app questions.

  4. Phase 2 budget

    About $45 (≈ ₱2,800) a month from 1,000 members, or earlier for daily backups.

  5. Legal review

    Lawyer and Data Protection Officer approve the Terms, Community Rules and Privacy Notice.
