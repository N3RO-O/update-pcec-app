The two hardening migrations and upload-file function are absent from the live inventory. Existing direct storage policies bypass the planned upload gate.

Next step: Coordinate a tested migration, function and client rollout. Do not apply migrations blindly over custom production changes.

04Session and recovery rules miss the requested standardLive settingMajor

Absolute and idle session limits are both zero (never). Access tokens last one hour; email links expire at 3,600 seconds, rather than less than an hour.

Next step: Agree supported server session deadlines and shorter recovery expiry; test logout and password-change revocation.

05Login and bot protection need tighteningLive settingMajor

The observed sign-in/sign-up limit is 30 per five minutes per IP. This does not meet five attempts per 15 minutes per IP and address; CAPTCHA is off.

06Profile photos can be shared outside the appLive settingMajor
