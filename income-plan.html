PCEC Connect · Income planning · 5 October 2026

How PCEC Connect
can fund its work.

A practical starting plan: make renewals easier, offer useful training, and give churches a clear way to support the app’s running costs.

Proposal for discussion. No new fees have been approved.
All example amounts are planning figures. This page collects no payments, starts no subscriptions and changes no member access.
Recommended starting point

Keep everyday member access free.

Start with renewal reminders and one paid training pilot. Invite voluntary support for the app only after PCEC agrees on its purpose, amount and handling.

The first goal is to cover confirmed operating costs. Choose a monthly budget from actual collections before increasing paid services.

Four ways to bring in funds

  1. 01

    Make membership renewals easier

    Remind churches when dues are due, show official payment instructions, and let the office record confirmed payments and receipts.

    This helps collect existing PCEC income. It is not a new charge for using the app. PCEC’s published 2022 membership form describes annual dues; the current approved rates still need confirmation.

    Start hereConfirm current dues, renewal dates and the person checking payments.
  2. 02

    Offer paid training and events

    Use the app to announce a PCEC-approved workshop or conference, manage registrations and guide attendees through payment.

    Ticket collections are income per event. Deduct speakers, venue, materials, payment fees and other event expenses before deciding what can support the app. Attendance and certificates would need additional work.

    Small pilotChoose one useful event and approve its price and expenses first.
  3. 03

    Invite voluntary app support

    Participating churches could make a monthly contribution specifically toward keeping PCEC Connect running.

    For example, 20 churches contributing ₱250 each would provide ₱5,000 a month before expenses. Participation and the amount are optional proposals, not an existing fee or a forecast.

    Optional supportExplain what the funds cover and provide a clear contribution record.
  4. 04

    Find an operating-cost sponsor

    A PCEC-approved partner could fund hosting, email delivery or member support for an agreed period.

    Agree on the amount, duration and any acknowledgement before relying on that support. A one-time sponsorship should not be counted as recurring monthly income.

    Additional fundingAssign someone to discuss terms and track the agreed support.

A small pilot before a bigger commitment

  1. Prepare

    Agree on the details and finish payment handling

    Confirm official fees, payment destinations, receipts, payment checking and any event cancellation or refund arrangements. Finish the live security and email setup. The current app still needs reliable payment confirmation and receipt tracking.

  2. Pilot

    Test renewals and one paid training event

    Invite a small group, explain the process and have the office verify each payment. Avoid assuming that every app user represents a paying church.

  3. Review

    Compare actual collections with actual costs

    Review participation, unpaid registrations, fees, event expenses, app costs and staff time. Decide whether voluntary support or a sponsor is needed before expanding.

What PCEC needs to decide

Funding and accountability

  • Which income source to pilot first.
  • Current membership rates and any proposed event price.
  • Who verifies payments and issues receipts.
  • How much collected income is allocated to the app.
  • The approved monthly budget and administrator alert recipient.

Developer compensation

A setup fee and a monthly maintenance fee can be discussed separately with PCEC. Agree on support hours, included updates, responsibilities and payment terms.

The app’s funding does not automatically become the developer’s personal income. Include any agreed maintenance cost in the operating budget.

A later option, if churches ask for it: paid church-management tools such as attendance, exports or certificate handling. These would need new features, a clear benefit and a pricing pilot. They are not included in the starting plan or income example.
